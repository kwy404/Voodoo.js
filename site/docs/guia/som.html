Interface and effects

Sound

Someone clicks a button and cannot tell whether anything happened. A short blip settles that better than any animation. Here the effects are synthesized on the spot, with the Web Audio API: there is no file to download, there is no sounds folder, and the whole module costs about 2 KB.

In the HTML

<div class="linha">
  <button v-sound="click">click</button>
  <button v-sound="success">success</button>
  <button v-sound="error">error</button>
  <button v-sound="coin">coin</button>
  <button v-mute>Mute</button>
</div>

With no argument, v-sound plays on click. With an argument, you choose the event: 

<button v-sound="success" v-post="/api/orders">Check out</button>
<a v-sound:mouseenter="hover" href="/pricing">Pricing</a>
<input v-sound:input="type">

And v-mute gives the visitor the mute button, which is the first thing they will look for.

From JavaScript, and through $sound

V.sound.play('success');
V.sound.play('/audio/ding.mp3');    // it takes a file too
V.sound.note('a', 300);             // a musical note, with an optional octave: 'a5'
V.sound.melody(['c', 'e', 'g'], 140);
V.sound.tone(440, 200);             // frequency in hertz
V.sound.volume(0.4);
V.sound.mute();
V.sound.toggle();
<div class="linha">
  <button @click="$sound.play('levelup')">Level up</button>
  <button @click="$sound.melody(['c', 'e', 'g'], 140)">Melody</button>
  <button @click="$sound.note('a', 300)">The note A</button>
  <button @click="$sound.toggle()">On and off</button>
</div>

Three courtesies the module already takes care of

  • The audio context is only born on the person's first gesture. No browser allows it earlier, and Voodoo does not try. A sound fired on load simply does not play.
  • Anyone who turns on the reduced-motion preference in their system gets a lower volume.
  • The choice of volume and of silence is kept between visits, so nobody has to mute the same page twice.

Defining an effect of your own

V.sound.define('myAlert', {
  volume: 0.5,
  camadas: [{ frequencia: 700, duracao: 0.1 }]
});
Interface sound is there to confirm

Not to grab attention. A 40-millisecond click goes unnoticed and still makes the interface feel solid; a long sound on every action wears thin by the third time. When in doubt, use less — and remember that the person may be in an office, in a class, or with someone asleep next to them.

The sound module ships in voodoo.min.js and in voodoo.full.min.js.
