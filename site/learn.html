Interactive lesson

Learn Voodoo in 10 minutes

Eight short steps, each one a small task in a live editor. Type, watch the result change, and the step checks itself. Nothing to install.

  • About 10 minutes
  • 8 short steps
  • Progress stays in this browser

    Step 1 of 8

    Say hello

    A Voodoo page is plain HTML. v-data holds the state of a block, and anything in braces, like { name }, is shown on the page and kept up to date.

    Your turn

    Change 'Vudu' to your own name.

    Edit the code. The step checks itself as you type.
